yocable data deletion

the reference version of this text is now at https://yolointeractive.app/en/supprimer-mes-donnees/. this page is a copy, kept so that older links keep working.

application: yocable — learn vocabulary (com.yolointeractive.yocable), by yolo interactive llc.

delete your account from the application

one screen of the application deletes the account and everything with it.

  1. open yocable on your phone;
  2. open Options;
  3. choose Data Deletion Instructions;
  4. press Delete Account Permanently;
  5. type DELETE to confirm.

the same screen can also be opened here: https://yocable.yolointeractive.app/delete-data/.

delete your account by mail

if you would rather write, send a message to contact@yolointeractive.app, saying which application and which account address. the deletion is done within one month, and confirmed in writing.

what is deleted, and what is kept

the account and everything attached to it are deleted. the deletion is immediate. a backup copy may survive seven days, then it disappears on its own. nothing is kept after that, and nothing is kept for any other purpose.

what lives in your phone alone — your progress, your settings — goes with the application when you uninstall it.
