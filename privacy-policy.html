yocable privacy policy

the reference version of this text is now at https://yolointeractive.app/en/confidentialite/. this page is a copy, kept so that older links keep working.

nothing about you is kept, and every sentence on this page is measured.

this site

it collects nothing.

no cookie is placed in your browser. no script comes from anywhere else. there is no form and no account. the server log keeps neither your address nor anything that points to you: only the date, the page asked for, and the answer code.

pages read are counted. people are not, and this site has no way of doing so.

each of these four sentences is checked at every release.

yocable

no account, and no internet.

the application works entirely offline. its words ship with it, and your progress lives in your phone. no account is needed to use it.

if you create an account, it stores seven things: your identifier, your mail address, your display name, your picture, your points, your tier and your language pairs. the server rule lets you alone read them, and refuses everything else.

nothing is shared with a third party. there is no audience measurement and no tracker.

the other three

they are not open yet.

yotrade is private and by invitation. yomind is being built. yotime is coming. their texts will be written when they open.
